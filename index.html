The story

Families caring for an elderly parent need the right equipment fast, without calling around or waiting on replies. Elderlymed's store lets them browse walkers, commode chairs and health monitors in Arabic or English, see what's in stock, and order in a few taps. They pay cash on delivery, with a flat 2 JD fee to every governorate in Jordan. Behind the scenes, the team runs products, orders, customer messages and reviews from one simple dashboard.

  • Cash on delivery
  • 2 JD to every governorate
  • Arabic & English
  • One dashboard for every order
